## 1. Institutional identity

HIACDI Tech Hub is a Kenya-based centre for technology education, applied practice, and digital solutions. The institution trains high school leavers, university students, and working professionals who want a serious path into software, data, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and related digital work.

Teaching is live, reviewed, and built around real output. HIACDI Tech Hub is not a content library. Learners study through instructor-led classes, laboratory time with a mentor, and project reviews that test whether a skill can be applied — not whether a recording was watched.

The institutional motto is **Learn. Build. Innovate.** The public tagline is **Technology Education + Innovation + Digital Solutions.** The guiding statement of the Hub is to empower the next generation of digital innovators: people are met where they are and guided to where they want to go in the technology sector.

---

## 2. Purpose and academic standard

The standard of the Hub is simple. Learners should understand the craft, produce work they can defend, and leave ready for employment, further study, or building their own products.

Three pillars organise the academic culture:

**Learn — instruction with a standard.**  
Programmes are designed by practitioners. Study proceeds through live classes, guided labs, and assessments. Full-time, part-time, remote, and in-person options are offered according to the programme.

**Build — projects that can be shown.**  
Every pathway is project-based. Mentors review code, analysis, and decisions. The aim is a portfolio of finished work: applications, datasets, security reports, and products that a learner can walk an employer through.

**Innovate — skill that solves a problem.**  
Technology is treated as a tool for opportunity. Learners practise on problems that resemble the workplace, then carry those habits into internships, teams, startups, and community projects across the HIACDI ecosystem.

## 4. Teaching model

Mentorship is how HIACDI Tech Hub teaches. Classes combine live walkthroughs, pair work, and project reviews so a learner understands why a solution works and then rebuilds it independently.

The teaching model includes:

- live instructor-led sessions, not slide-only delivery;
- laboratory time with a mentor in the room or on the call;
- code, data, and security reviews before work is marked complete;
- career studio support for portfolios, interviews, and next steps.

This approach is designed for people starting out and for people switching into technology. A university degree is not required for most programmes. A laptop, basic computer use, and commitment to attend class and complete assignments are the usual prerequisites; each course page states who the programme is for.

## 7. Tuition and installment

Tuition is set per intake and displayed on the relevant course page. The current published structure is:

- **Total tuition:** Ksh 21,000  
- **Monthly installment:** Ksh 3,500 per month  

A learner may pay the full amount before class begins, or pay in monthly installments of Ksh 3,500 until the total is cleared (six months at the current figures). The first monthly payment is due on or before class start. A place is confirmed when HIACDI Tech Hub receives the full tuition or the first monthly installment.

Payment instructions are issued after an application is accepted. Enquiries: hiacditechhub@gmail.com or WhatsApp 0741 808 582.

Admissions may revise the total fee and the monthly amount for a future intake. Course pages and the tuition payment guide always show the figures in force for that intake.

## 8. Admissions

**Who may apply.** High school leavers, university students, and working professionals. Programmes serve both beginners and career switchers.

**How to apply.** Create a learner account on the HIACDI Tech Hub website, open Apply while applications are open, choose the program area, preferred course, and learning mode, and complete the application. Applications are accepted only during the intake window set by the academic office.

**Admissions calls.** Candidates who wish to speak with Academic Advisors before applying may book a group information session (typically 45 minutes, East Africa Time). Sessions cover courses, learning models, payment options, and career questions. A laptop or smartphone, a stable connection, and a quiet location are required. Join with video on and microphone muted unless speaking.

**Contact for admissions.** hiacditechhub@gmail.com · WhatsApp 0741 808 582 · website Contact Us form.

## 9. Certificates and verification

Learners who complete an awarded programme receive a HIACDI Tech Hub certificate. Only graduates entered in the institutional register can be verified.

To verify a certificate, scan the QR code on the document or use **Verify a certificate** on the website, then enter the registered full name and email. If the details do not match an awarded graduate, confirmation is not issued.

## 10. Corporate, partners, and talent

HIACDI Tech Hub works with universities, companies, NGOs, and public institutions on:

- **Staff training** — custom programmes in software, data, cybersecurity, and AI for teams that need skill they can use in the near term.
- **Capacity building** — labs, mentor models, and pathways designed to last after a workshop ends.
- **Talent pipelines** — hiring from graduates who have been reviewed on real projects, or a sponsored cohort for an organisation.
- **Digital solutions** — applied builds in which learners and practitioners work on a defined problem to a professional standard.

Partnership enquiries: hiacditechhub@gmail.com, subject line “HIACDI Tech Hub partnership”.

The Hub also invites practitioners who can teach and review work to express interest in technical mentor and academic facilitator roles in software engineering, data and AI, cybersecurity, and related fields.
